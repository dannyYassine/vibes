# Monthly Spending Summary

## Description
As a user, I want to see a monthly summary of my spending broken down by category so I can track my budget.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Dashboard shows total income, total expense, and net for the current month
2. Each category shows amount and percentage of total spending
3. Categories are color-coded for quick scanning
4. Summary updates automatically after new transactions are synced

## Priority
medium

## Stakeholder
End user
